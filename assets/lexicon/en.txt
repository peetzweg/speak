# speak's built-in English pronunciations: words from developer and agent text that misaki's
# dictionaries don't know, or read in a way nobody says them. Same format and matching as the
# user lexicon (`word = spoken form`, whole words, ignoring case, longest first); the user's own
# entry for a word replaces the one here. English voices only.
#
# A spoken form is a respelling misaki's dictionaries read by themselves, so the word sounds the
# same with or without espeak-ng and follows the voice's accent (US or GB):
#   * capitals apart (`C H mod`) or with dots (`A.W.S.`) are spelled as letters,
#   * a hyphen makes one word with one main stress (`e-num`, `T-mux`),
#   * `/phonemes/` (Kokoro's alphabet) only where no respelling works, and `[piece](/phonemes/)`
#     for one piece of a respelling; both use sounds US and GB share where they can.
# An entry is here only if one of speak's readings (espeak-ng or misaki's network, US or GB)
# gets the word wrong. Never an ordinary English word, or a word whose other casing is one with
# a different reading. Lines starting `# contested:` mark readings people disagree on.
